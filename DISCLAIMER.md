# Disclaimer

This repository contains document templates, checklists and reference notes for
Pakistani companies and individuals exporting information technology services.

**It is not legal, tax, accounting or financial advice, and no professional
relationship is created by using it.**

The maintainers are software engineers, not tax practitioners.

## Specifically

- Tax rates, withholding rates, thresholds and exemptions in Pakistan change
  with each Finance Act, and several changed within the last twelve months.
- State Bank of Pakistan procedures for export receipts, retention accounts and
  declarations are amended by circular, sometimes with little notice, and
  implementation varies between banks and even between branches.
- Registration requirements and fees for PSEB, SECP, FBR and P@SHA change
  between cycles.
- Nothing here accounts for your particular facts. The right answer for a
  single-member company invoicing one overseas client is not the right answer
  for a firm with domestic and export revenue and staff.

## What to do instead of relying on this

Verify anything you are about to file, pay for or sign against the relevant
official source at the time you act on it:

- **SECP** — secp.gov.pk and eservices.secp.gov.pk
- **FBR** — fbr.gov.pk and the IRIS portal
- **State Bank of Pakistan** — sbp.org.pk, for circulars on export receipts and
  foreign currency accounts
- **PSEB** — pseb.org.pk
- **Your own bank**, in writing, for anything about how they will treat a
  payment

And engage a practising chartered accountant or tax practitioner before your
first domestic invoice, before your first year-end, and before any transaction
with a company you are connected to.

The cost of one consultation is trivial against the cost of getting the first
year wrong.
