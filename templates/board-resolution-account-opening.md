# Board resolution — bank account opening

Banks require a resolution authorising the account and naming who may operate
it. For a single member company, a decision recorded in the minutes book and
signed by the sole director is generally sufficient — but take a printed copy on
letterhead to the branch, because that is what they will file.

Replace everything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]`.

---

**[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]**

## Resolution for opening and operating a bank account

`[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]` · CUIN `[0000000]`

Extract of the resolution passed at a meeting of the `[board of directors / sole
director]` of `[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]` held on `[DATE]` at `[REGISTERED OFFICE
ADDRESS]`, duly recorded in the minutes book of the company.

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT:**

1. A current account in the name of **`[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`** be opened with
   `[BANK NAME]` at its `[BRANCH NAME]` branch, `[CITY]`.

2. The said bank be and is hereby authorised to honour cheques, bills of
   exchange and promissory notes drawn, signed, accepted or made on behalf of
   the company, and to act on any instructions given in respect of any account
   of the company, whether in credit or overdrawn.

3. **`[FULL NAME]`**, holder of CNIC `[00000-0000000-0]`, `[DESIGNATION]`, be
   and is hereby authorised to operate the said account **`[singly / jointly
   with ___]`**, and to sign all instruments and instructions in connection with
   it.

4. `[FULL NAME]` be and is hereby authorised to complete all account opening
   formalities with the bank, including the execution of any account opening
   forms, mandates, declarations and specimen signature cards.

5. The company apply for `[an Exporters' Special Foreign Currency Account in
   USD / other accounts as required]` at the same branch, and for a debit card
   against the balance held in it.

6. The company subscribe to the bank's internet banking service, with
   `[FULL NAME]` as authorised signatory and security token holder.

7. The declaration as to the nature of the company's export services and the
   applicable inward remittance purpose code be filed with the bank.

**FURTHER RESOLVED THAT** the above resolution be communicated to `[BANK NAME]`
and shall constitute the mandate of the company to the bank, and shall remain in
force until revoked in writing or any change is intimated by the company.

Certified to be a true extract from the minutes.

<br>

`[SIGNATURE]`

**`[FULL NAME]`**
`[DESIGNATION]`
For and on behalf of `[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`

Date: `[DATE]`
