# Purpose code declaration

A one-time letter to your bank declaring the nature of the services you export,
so that inbound foreign currency is realised under the IT services export code
rather than a personal or home remittance code.

**Give this to the branch before your first client payment arrives.** Take two
copies, have one stamped as received, and keep it. Ask for the name and direct
number of the officer who handles foreign remittances.

---

## Why it matters

When foreign currency arrives in Pakistan, the receiving bank assigns a purpose
code classifying what the money is for. IT and IT-enabled services exports have
their own code, distinct from the code used for family remittances and gifts.

If a client payment is coded as a personal remittance:

- No withholding happens at realisation. This feels like a win for about a week.
  It is not — it means the income was never recorded as export income.
- Your entitlement to concessional treatment on that income becomes something
  you have to argue for rather than evidence.
- The payment does not count toward your IT export turnover for PSEB renewal or
  any turnover-based facility.
- A pattern of substantial regular inflows coded as family gifts is exactly the
  profile that draws scrutiny.

Under the State Bank's 2026 measures for IT exporters, a one-time declaration of
the nature of services exported replaces per-transaction documentation for most
receipts, and banks tag the relevant service and purpose code against the
exporter's account. This letter is that declaration.

## Template

Copy onto your company letterhead. Replace everything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]`.

---

**[YOUR COMPANY LETTERHEAD]**

Date: `[DATE]`

The Branch Manager
`[BANK NAME]`
`[BRANCH NAME AND ADDRESS]`

**Subject: Declaration of the nature of export services — inward remittance purpose code**

Dear Sir / Madam,

`[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`, incorporation number `[CUIN]`, holder of account
number `[ACCOUNT NUMBER]` / IBAN `[IBAN]` at your branch, is an exporter of
information technology and IT-enabled services.

The services we export are: software development, web and application
development, systems integration, software consultancy, technical support and
related information technology services, supplied to clients outside Pakistan.

`[IF APPLICABLE:]` We are registered with the Pakistan Software Export Board,
registration number `[PSEB REGISTRATION NUMBER]`. A copy of the certificate is
enclosed.

In accordance with the State Bank of Pakistan's measures for information
technology exporters, we provide this one-time declaration of the nature of the
services we export, and request that:

1. This account be tagged with the relevant service and purpose code for
   information technology services, so that all inbound foreign currency
   received in it is realised under the IT services export purpose code and not
   under any home remittance, personal transfer or gift code.

2. A Proceeds Realisation Certificate or Inward Remittance Certificate be issued
   to us for every export receipt credited to this account.

3. We are advised of the name and contact details of the officer responsible for
   foreign remittances at this branch, so that any coding query can be raised
   and resolved promptly.

We confirm that all foreign currency received in this account represents
proceeds from the export of information technology services rendered by the
company to clients outside Pakistan, and that we will notify you promptly of any
change in the nature of our business.

Yours faithfully,

`[SIGNATURE]`

`[FULL NAME]`
`[DESIGNATION]`
For and on behalf of `[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`

**Enclosures:** `[Certificate of Incorporation / PSEB certificate / NTN certificate]`

## After you hand it in

- Get one copy stamped as received and keep it with your company records.
- Note the remittance officer's name and number somewhere you will find it again.
- Put the instruction line on every invoice as well, so the *sending* bank's
  reference field states what the money is for before it arrives. See
  [`invoice-template.html`](invoice-template.html).
- Check the credit advice for every payment. See
  [`../checklists/per-payment.md`](../checklists/per-payment.md).

If the branch is unfamiliar with the current procedure, ask them to check with
their trade finance or remittance desk rather than accepting a refusal at the
counter. Implementation across branches has been uneven.
