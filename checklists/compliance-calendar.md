# Compliance calendar

Everything recurring. Put all of it in a calendar with two reminders — thirty
days out and seven days out — today.

> Deadlines differ by entity type, financial year end and province. Confirm
> yours with your accountant. See [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md).

---

## One-off, but with hard deadlines

| When | Obligation | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| Within 30 days of incorporation | Registered office intimation | Registrar |
| Within 30 days of incorporation | Subscription money deposited into the company account | Company / bank |
| Within 45 days of incorporation | Chartered accountant's certificate of receipt of subscription money, filed | Registrar |
| Within 90 days of incorporation | Appoint the first auditor | Company record |

## Annual

| When | Obligation |
|---|---|
| After financial year end | Company income tax return. **File even if there is no tax to pay** — not filing removes you from the Active Taxpayers List, which breaks the concessional export rate. |
| After financial year end | Annual return to the registrar |
| After financial year end | Audited financial statements |
| On the certificate anniversary | PSEB renewal. Start 30–45 days early; a lapse means the concessional rate does not apply to income received during the gap. |
| Fixed date, regardless of join date | Trade body membership renewal, if joined |
| Set auto-renew | Domain renewals |
| Personal deadline | Your own income tax return, separate from the company's |

## Monthly or quarterly

| When | Obligation |
|---|---|
| Monthly | Withdraw payment platform balances to the Pakistani account — repatriation discipline |
| Monthly | Archive the bank statement. Do not rely on being able to pull twelve months of history later |
| Monthly, by the statutory date | Withholding tax statements, once you are a withholding agent |
| Monthly or quarterly | Sales tax on services returns, if domestic revenue triggers registration |

## Per event

| Trigger | Obligation |
|---|---|
| Every inbound export payment | Check the purpose code on the credit advice; request the PRC. [Checklist](per-payment.md) |
| Every domestic invoice | Obtain the withholding tax certificate from the client |
| Change of directors, address or share capital | Notify the registrar within the prescribed period |
| Always | Maintain statutory registers and the minutes book |

## The cheapest insurance you can buy

A corporate compliance retainer from a company secretary or small firm covers
the annual return, statutory registers and reminders for a modest annual fee.
For a single founder, that is the difference between compliance being handled
and compliance being something you discover you missed. Buy it once revenue
starts.
