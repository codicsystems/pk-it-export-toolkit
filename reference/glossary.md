# Glossary

The acronyms, explained once.

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **ATL** | Active Taxpayers List. FBR's published list of taxpayers who have filed. Being on it roughly halves withholding rates across the board and is a prerequisite for several things below. Falling off it because you did not file a return in a year with no tax to pay is a common and expensive mistake. |
| **CUIN** | Corporate Universal Identification Number. The seven-digit number SECP assigns to a company at incorporation. The primary company identifier in Pakistan, and what anyone verifying you will search. |
| **ESFCA** | Exporters' Special Foreign Currency Account. Lets a registered exporter hold a permitted share of export proceeds in foreign currency rather than converting everything to rupees on arrival. Useful for paying foreign costs — hosting, tooling, subcontractors — without converting twice. |
| **FBR** | Federal Board of Revenue. Pakistan's tax authority. Its online portal is IRIS. |
| **IRC** | Inward Remittance Certificate. What some banks call a PRC. Same purpose. |
| **NTN** | National Tax Number. Your tax registration number with FBR. A company's NTN is separate from its director's personal NTN, and it is generated from the incorporation record because SECP and FBR are integrated. |
| **P@SHA** | Pakistan Software Houses Association. The industry trade body. Membership runs to 31 March regardless of when you join, which is worth knowing before you pay in September. |
| **PRC** | Proceeds Realisation Certificate. Bank-issued proof that a specific foreign payment was realised, for what amount, on what date, under which purpose code. **Your legal proof of export income.** Ask for one for every export receipt. |
| **PSEB** | Pakistan Software Export Board. Registration is not legally mandatory but is the difference between operating as a recognised IT exporter and as an anonymous recipient of foreign remittances. It gates the concessional tax rate, foreign currency retention, the national IT directory listing, visa facilitation letters and subsidised expo participation. |
| **Purpose code** | The classification a bank assigns to an inbound foreign payment. See [purpose-codes.md](purpose-codes.md) — this is the one that costs people money. |
| **SECP** | Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. The company registrar. Maintains the public register where anyone can verify that your company exists. |
| **SMC** | Single Member Company. A private limited company with one shareholder. Requires a nominee — a succession mechanism required by law, not a shareholding, and not a co-founder. |
| **STZA** | Special Technology Zones Authority. Administers designated technology zones with their own tax and regulatory treatment. Relevant once you have staff and a physical office. |
| **Zero-rated** | Charged at 0% sales tax, as distinct from exempt. Exported IT services are normally zero-rated. Domestic services are a different question and depend on where you are registered. |
